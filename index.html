What to Review Before Choosing a Supplement

A responsible decision begins with transparent information and realistic expectations.

Read the Label

Review the complete ingredient list, serving directions, warnings, and manufacturer details before making a purchase.

Check the Terms

Confirm the current price, shipping conditions, refund policy, subscription terms, and customer-service information.

Ask a Professional

Speak with a qualified healthcare professional before using a supplement, especially when taking medication or managing a medical condition.
